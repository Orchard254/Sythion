**What the pair of new tests does and does not pin - stated plainly:**

* `test_the_clean_settings_snapshot_survives_an_in_place_mutation` **DOES** pin "the shared clean

  nothing to differ, so it passes whether or not the isolation exists.
* Together, the pair pins the *behaviour* of a clean checkout (the suite sees clean values) and the
  *shape* of the isolation (per-test copies, no shared mutable snapshot). It does **not** pin the
  *existence* of the isolation in a clean checkout. **A CI runner cannot detect a deleted
  isolation with the tests as they stand.** This is the Reviewer's finding 1, and I confirm it
  rather than dispute it.
