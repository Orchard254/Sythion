## Invariant

- Invariant: `max_specialists_per_task` (default 2) is never exceeded at the
  admission boundary. Enforcement lives in the single conditional
  `UPDATE ... (SELECT COUNT(*) ...) < :limit` (`_ADMIT_SQL`) whose writer-lock
  serialization on separate connections is the cross-process guarantee; the
  in-process `_admission_lock` (RLock) is a best-effort fast path only and is
  NOT the guarantee. The idempotent already-`VALIDATED` short-circuit is
  deliberately not folded into the count predicate.
- Verified by: 20/20 isolated passes of the per-thread file-backed boundary
  test (cap never exceeded), 20/20 passes of the shared-writer file-backed
  boundary test, 1/1 pass of the cross-process admission test, plus the
  retained in-memory diagnostic case documenting the shared-connection harness
  contract.
