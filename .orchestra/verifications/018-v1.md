Mechanism in the installed **Starlette 0.35.1** (source read, not assumed):
`ServerErrorMiddleware.__call__` sends the handler's response and then
**always** re-raises (`# We always continue to raise the exception.` ->
`raise exc`); `_TestClientTransport.handle_request` re-raises only
`if self.raise_server_exceptions:`. The two client modes therefore differ
exactly as the paragraph now states. The paragraph no longer claims the 409
and the propagating exception co-occur (that was r1's blocking F1), and each
half is now pinned by one of the commit's own tests.
